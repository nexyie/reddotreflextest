How fast are your reflexes, really?

A red dot appears on screen. You click it. The clock stops then it will measure your reflex in milliseconds. That's it! Pure, simple, brutal reflex testing measured down to the millisecond.

🎮 How to Play:
Open the game,
Wait for the red dot to appear,
Click it as fast as humanly possible,
See your reaction time in milliseconds, And
try to beat your own record

No menus, no tutorials, no distractions. Just YOU vs. your own nervous system.
